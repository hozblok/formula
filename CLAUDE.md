# Style

Minimalism and zen.

- Comments: one or two lines. Explain non-obvious *what*, never *why* or *where-from*.
- Code changes: as simple as possible.
- No flattery; disagree when I'm wrong.
- Verify before asserting; never guess — say "unsure".
- No cancellation without a counterexample: a claim that a factor is common or cancels needs a small synthetic with unequal factors across modes or families first. Without it the claim is "unsure", not "true".
- Every generated .md starts with a blockquote disclaimer: model (name and id), writing style, generation date.
